    <p>The University of Cincinnati’s academic program catalog, including undergraduate and graduate programs both on campus and online, is published by the Office of the Provost.</p>
<p>The catalog provides descriptions of every active program, including an overview of the program, admission requirements, career outlook, and accreditation. A curriculum map is also included, detailing the requirements needed to complete the program.</p>
<p>Changes will be made to this academic record only to correct errors. This catalog is a static snapshot of the program descriptions and curriculum maps as they are published at the time of capture. Every effort has been made to ensure the accuracy of information.</p>
