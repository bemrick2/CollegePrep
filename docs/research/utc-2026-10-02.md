## Gaps

- **Admissions metrics.** The official Common Data Set 2025-2026 PDFs (2026-06 and 2026-09 uploads) came back with blank form fields. No admissions metrics are recorded.
- **Catalog pages.** The 2026-2027 catalog (catalog.utc.edu, catoid=53) returned empty content: the degree and graduation requirements, academic regulations, and program pages. As a result:
  - The minimum transfer grade, the two-year-college hour cap and the final-hours residence rule are unknown.
  - Program requirement groups (major core, choose-N lists) are not yet structured. Only the sheets' totals and term plans are recorded.
- **Other credit and aid policies.** No IB, Cambridge or dual-enrollment policy was found yet. No special-circumstances (professional judgment) process was found.
- **Freshman admission page.** It returned a server error (520).

## Correction (2026-10-03)

Research pipeline run `2026-10-03-catalog` fetched the three Clear Path PDFs and stored their verbatim text. That text shows the earlier summarizing read put some courses in the wrong term:

- **CS Data Science & AI:** ENGL 1020 belongs in Year 1 Spring, not Fall. CPEN 3700 and MATH 2030/3000 belong in Year 2 Spring, not Fall.
- **Psychology:** one "Elective (3)" belongs in Year 4 Spring, not Fall.

After the correction, every value in the UTC program and requirement rows was checked against the verbatim text. Each source is labeled "2026-2027", so those rows are now `verified`. The UTC cost, award, credit, transfer and appeal records stay `partially_verified` until the pipeline re-reads them.
