## What happens next

1. **Content lead and reviewers are appointed** (owner; nothing below can start without them).
2. The content lead records reviewers (`reviewers.json`), confirms tracks, and chooses reserve candidates per exam
   and section (`requested_pool` is a request only).
3. Review runs per item, stages 3–8, with these packets. Sign-offs are recorded with `recordEditorial.ts --registry`.
   Only individually approved items become eligible.
4. Publication and the reserve stay held while hosted is paused (`LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md` A5, H2).
