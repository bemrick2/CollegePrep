# National school baseline: completeness (2023-24 IPEDS collection)

Generated by `python3 scripts/build_baseline.py`. Rows: `baseline/schools/2023-24/<ST>.csv`; field definitions: `baseline/FIELDS.json`; universe: `docs/coverage/baseline/UNIVERSE.md`.

- **In-scope schools: 2,119** (Every four-year school in pipeline/registry/<ST>.json (docs/coverage/baseline/UNIVERSE.md)).
- **Complete baseline: 2,004** (every essential field is verified_current, available_older or not_published_or_na, and the school is listed active in the provisional 2024-25 directory); 0 with every field current or not applicable.
- **Closed, inactive or missing in the provisional 2024-25 directory: 19** (still listed and flagged in `UNIVERSE.md`; never counted complete or current).
- **Enrollment-weighted:** 98.5% of 10,533,478 undergraduates (fall 2023) attend a school with a complete baseline; 3 schools have no reported enrollment and weigh zero.
- A complete baseline is historical official data for comparison. IPEDS prices are 2023-24 and net prices are 2022-23 averages, so they are `available_older`, never current 2026-27 prices or personal estimates.

## Policy layer (separate measure)
